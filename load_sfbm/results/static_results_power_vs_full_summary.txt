2. WHY DO POWERGRAPHICS AND FULL GRAPHICS SHOW DIFFERENT STRESS VALUES?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Commands:
- PowerGraphics:  /GRAPHICS, POWER  (ANSYS GUI Default)
- Full Graphics:  /GRAPHICS, FULL   (Classical APDL Batch Mode)

1. Root Cause in Structural Modeling (/ESHAPE, 1 & BEAM188):
   In the parametric finite element model, the structural skin, spar webs, and rib webs are modeled
   using SHELL181 elements, while the spanwise stringers and spar caps are modeled using BEAM188 elements.
   When /ESHAPE, 1 is active, 1D beam elements are rendered with true 3D cross-sectional geometry.

2. Mechanism of Divergence:
   - In Full Graphics (/GRAPHICS, FULL):
     MAPDL follows strict classical element-to-node averaging. However, by APDL mathematical design:
     "BEAM/PIPE elements are detected in the selected element set. They are ignored in the nodal averaging."
     Because beam elements do not share 2D stress tensors with shell elements, Full Graphics excludes
     the beam cross-section stress from PLNSOL and NSORT. Consequently, Full Graphics reports ONLY the
     continuous shell membrane/bending stress in the skins and spar webs (12.50 MPa to 15.47 MPa).

   - In PowerGraphics (/GRAPHICS, POWER - Default in ANSYS GUI):
     PowerGraphics calculates and displays stresses based on external geometric facets. With /ESHAPE, 1,
     it generates 3D surface facets for BEAM188 stringers and flanges.
     At the clamped cantilever root (Y = 0), the maximum bending moment (M_root = -16,058 N*m) induces
     high localized axial tension/compression in the outer stringer and spar flange cross-sections.
     PowerGraphics captures these localized beam section facet stresses, yielding the true screen SMX:
     * Config 1: 29.54 MPa  (matches cfg1_vonmises_seqv.png)
     * Config 2: 45.73 MPa  (matches cfg2_vonmises_seqv.png)
     * Config 3: 59.45 MPa  (matches cfg3_vonmises_seqv.png)

3. DMX VS. TIP_UZ: COMPREHENSIVE ENGINEERING CLARIFICATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: "สรุปเราต้องดู DMX หรือ TIP_UZ ในการรายงานผล?"

1. Definitions:
   - DMX (Displacement Vector Sum Maximum):
     DMX = sqrt(UX^2 + UY^2 + UZ^2) >= 0
     Absolute spatial displacement magnitude of any node in the model.
   - TIP_UZ (Z-Axis Directional Displacement at Wingtip):
     TIP_UZ = UZ at y = SPAN (3978 mm) < 0
     True vertical bending deflection of the tailplane under downward aerodynamic loading.

2. Physical Proportion:
   - Vertical deflection |UZ| accounts for 99.24% of DMX (e.g. In Config 1: 6.716 mm vs 6.769 mm).
   - The minor 0.76% difference (~0.05 mm) is caused by Poisson shortening (UY) and chordwise
     dihedral sweep warping (UX).

3. Recommendation for Project Defense & Thesis:
   - State BOTH values clearly:
     * Use TIP_UZ (-6.72 mm to -7.04 mm) when discussing aircraft flight dynamics, elevator clearance,
       and aeroelastic bending stiffness.
     * Use DMX (6.77 mm to 7.10 mm) when referencing ANSYS GUI plot contour bars.
